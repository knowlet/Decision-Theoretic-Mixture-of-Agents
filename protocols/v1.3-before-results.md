Models are fixed by identity before scoring: legacy `[gpt_4o, claude35_haiku, gemini25_flash, gemma3_12b]`; replacement `[gpt5_2, claude45_sonnet, gemini3_flash, gemma3_27b]`.

Group-hashed train/development/test 60/20/20. Maximum three acquisitions. Primary assumed cost 0.01 per acquired response and deferral loss 0.25. Compare fixed single/panel, same-source static batch, myopic and Bellman control, disagreement/majority, plus prompt-based single/cumulative-score routing. Refresh budgets 50/200 non-test questions are charged four responses each. Replacement is not a chronological within-agent training trajectory. Binary Brier/ECE and group-paired 2000-bootstrap intervals; DICES is not probability-of-correctness or individual preference ground truth.

No CERA-MoA training, Jev API or fresh LLM inference is claimed. Cumulative-score controls test an architectural idea, not those products. Actual source hashes, deterministic reruns, observation isolation, missing-value accounting and fail-closed CI gates are required. Positive, null and negative findings all remain reportable.
